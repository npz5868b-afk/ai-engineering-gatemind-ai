# Part A — Analyse Existing AI Systems

**Work mode:** Group (4–5 students)  
**Status:** Observation sheet uploaded; reflection waived by lecturer

## Required work
1. Examine the AI system assigned to the group (ChatGPT, YouTube/Spotify recommendations, or Claude).
2. Run the specified tests or observations from the Topic 1 lab.
3. Collect **2–3 pieces of non-sensitive evidence** (e.g., screenshots, observed changes, refusal behavior, measured latency).
4. Discuss **at least three quality dimensions**, with measurement methods.
5. Describe specific strengths and weaknesses, **at least two Responsible AI risks**, and one practical improvement.
6. Complete the group observation sheet and prepare three findings for group sharing, if requested. **The lecturer has waived the reflection.**
